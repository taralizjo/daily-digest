# Daily Digest — September 20, 2026

## AI
- **Anthropic and Accenture launch $2B "embedded evaluator" AI safety partnership**: On September 18, Anthropic named Accenture's specialist unit Faculty as its first "embedded evaluator" — outside safety experts granted employee-level access inside Anthropic to red-team models and test safeguards continuously through training and deployment, rather than auditing only after release. Anthropic and Accenture each pledged at least $1 billion over five years to the effort. It's the first concrete step under CEO Dario Amodei's "Pace the Frontier" proposal published September 12; OpenAI's Sam Altman has said he'll match the commitment, but no other major lab has yet followed suit. The move marks the industry's biggest structural shift yet toward independent, real-time safety oversight instead of self-policing. Source: https://www.anthropic.com/news/accenture-embedded-evaluation
- **Anthropic revenue hits $100B annualized run rate, targets November IPO**: Anthropic's annualized revenue is on track to top $100 billion in 2026, up from $65 billion at the end of July and just $9 billion at the end of 2025, driven by enterprise adoption of Claude for coding and workplace automation, according to reporting that surfaced September 18. The six-year-old company is reportedly targeting an IPO as early as November, with marketing starting mid-October, at a valuation near $2 trillion and a raise of up to $100 billion — which would top SpaceX's record. If it proceeds, it would rank among the largest tech IPOs ever and place Anthropic among the ten largest US companies by market cap. Source: https://www.axios.com/2026/09/18/anthropic-100-billion-revenue
- **"Plugin4Shell" zero-click flaw disclosed across major AI coding agents**: Security researchers publicly disclosed "Plugin4Shell" on September 17-18, a zero-click remote-code-execution vulnerability in the plugin systems of Claude Code, OpenAI Codex, GitHub Copilot, and Gemini CLI. The flaw breaks SHA-pinning — agents check out a pinned commit but never verify the local checkout actually matches it — letting a compromised upstream plugin repo silently push malicious code to installations with no user action required. Anthropic patched it in Claude Code v2.1.179 and OpenAI in Codex CLI v0.146.0; Google has deprecated Gemini CLI without a fix, and Microsoft had not shipped a Copilot patch as of disclosure. It's described as the first major supply-chain vulnerability of the AI-agent ecosystem, exposing source code, cloud credentials, and production systems to attackers. Source: https://www.theregister.com/security/2026/09/17/ai-coding-agents-0-click-rce-flaw-could-hand-attackers-keys-to-the-kingdom/
- **Trump vows to create an "AI Force" and appoint an AI czar, calling safety warnings a "hoax"**: Trump announced on September 19-20 that he is standing up a new "AI Force" modeled on the Space Force and will soon name an AI czar, doubling down on his push to accelerate AI development with limited regulation. He offered no details on budget, structure, or placement within government, saying via Truth Social that Washington would "not in any way hinder or stifle" AI growth but would police "BAD" behavior through the existing justice system. The announcement follows Anthropic's Dario Amodei, OpenAI's Sam Altman, xAI's Elon Musk, and Google DeepMind's Demis Hassabis all publicly calling for slowing frontier AI development over safety risks — concerns Trump again dismissed as a "hoax." It sets up a widening rift between AI industry leadership, increasingly unified on safety, and the White House's accelerationist stance. Source: https://www.axios.com/2026/09/19/trump-ai-czar-space-force-safety

## World Affairs
- **Ukraine launches its largest drone attack of the war on Moscow, hitting a major oil refinery**: Russia's defense ministry said air defenses downed 1,110 Ukrainian drones overnight into September 20 across 19 regions plus Crimea — the largest overnight total this year, well above the previous high of 822 in August — with Moscow's mayor calling it the biggest attack on the capital yet. The Ukrainian General Staff said the main target was the Gazpromneft-Moscow refinery in Kapotnya, one of Russia's largest, which supplies roughly 40% of the city's fuel and caught fire; regional officials reported two to three deaths. The strike landed on the final day of Russia's parliamentary elections, amplifying its political impact. It underscores Ukraine's deepening campaign against Russian energy infrastructure even as US sanctions pressure escalates in parallel. Source: https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-20/moscow-refinery-hit-as-russia-reports-largest-2026-drone-attack
- **Trump signs sweeping new Russia sanctions law named for the late Sen. Lindsey Graham**: Trump signed the "Lindsey O. Graham Sanctioning Russia and Iran Act of 2026" into law on September 19, after it cleared the House on September 17 and the Senate last month. The law authorizes tariffs of up to 100% on the five largest buyers of Russian oil and gas, plus sanctions on Russia's energy and defense sectors, its "shadow fleet" of tankers, and Putin himself along with top officials and financial institutions; Trump has 30 days to implement most measures but retains broad authority to suspend them. Kremlin spokesman Dmitry Peskov said the sanctions would "complicate" efforts to reach a Ukraine peace settlement. The law arrives the same day as Ukraine's largest drone strike on Moscow, sharply raising pressure on the Kremlin on multiple fronts at once. Source: https://www.themoscowtimes.com/2026/09/19/trump-signs-sweeping-russia-sanctions-bill-a93750
- **US and Denmark strike deal giving Washington "permanent" security control over Greenland**: Trump announced on September 18-19 that the US and Denmark reached an agreement granting the US what he called "permanent control over security" in Greenland, a sharp de-escalation from his earlier repeated threats to seize the Danish territory by force. The deal expands the US military presence on the island and bars US adversaries from stationing troops or bases there without written US authorization, at no cost to Washington. Danish PM Mette Frederiksen said the agreement "recognises the sovereignty and territorial integrity" of Greenland and Denmark, and it's expected to be formally signed by the US, Denmark, and Greenland next week alongside the UN General Assembly session in New York. It resolves one of the most contentious flashpoints in US-European relations this year without direct confrontation. Source: https://www.npr.org/2026/09/19/g-s1-144158/us-and-denmark-reach-deal
