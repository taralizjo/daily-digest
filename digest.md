# Daily Digest — 2026-10-24

## AI

**Anthropic launches Claude Duet, a proactive always-on agent:** Anthropic released Duet as a research preview on Oct 21. It monitors a user's connected apps, sends a daily summary, and flags items that need a decision. It reportedly runs on Claude Opus 5.5 in its own virtual machine with a browser, and asks for approval before consequential actions such as deleting, paying, signing or sending. Access is by waitlist for US Pro and Max subscribers on iOS, Android and claude.ai. Coverage frames it as a rival to Meta's Muse and OpenAI's Dots, and Anthropic itself says it can misjudge urgency. Source: https://www.macrumors.com/2026/10/21/anthropic-claude-duet/

**Microsoft says China-aligned Storm-0940 used agentic AI in the ConnectWise supply-chain attack:** Microsoft attributed the intrusion campaign to Storm-0940, reporting that the group used ARTEX, an open-source pentest framework that works with any AI model, to automate reconnaissance and exfiltration. Sign-in attempts reached more than 2,250 tenants, and in some cases attackers went from targeting to privileged access in under three hours. Most attempts failed, but some achieved persistent cloud access in defense, energy, finance and government targets; Microsoft says its own systems were not compromised. It is among the clearest real-world cases of AI-automated intrusion at scale, and it raises pressure on defenders to automate detection and response. Source: https://kfgo.com/2026/10/21/chinese-linked-hackers-compromise-connectwise-target-hundreds-in-global-attack-microsoft-says/

**OpenAI reportedly nears a roughly $30B funding round:** Reports say OpenAI is in talks to raise about $30B, anchored by UAE-linked investors (including MGX) and BlackRock, following a $122B round at an $852B valuation in March. Reported valuations differ between outlets, from about $1.25T to $1.4T pre-money, and terms are not final or signed. If completed, it would be one of the largest private raises ever and would show continued investor appetite for frontier-model spending despite market jitters. Source: https://www.businesstoday.com.my/2026/10/06/openai-in-talks-for-us30-billion-round-with-uae-funds-blackrock/

**Meta shares fall sharply on AI spending and cash-flow worries:** Meta's stock dropped about 7% on Oct 23 as investors reacted to heavy AI capital spending (2026 guidance reported at $135–145B) and sharply weaker free cash flow. Chip stocks fell with it, with the semiconductor index reportedly down more than 6% and Nvidia down about 4%. Figures vary across outlets, and no source ties Nvidia's decline directly to Meta. The episode tests how much patience markets have for AI infrastructure spending without matching near-term returns. Source: https://github.com/diclogic/ai-daily-digest/issues/191

## World Affairs

**Netanyahu orders West Bank lockdown after Hebron attack:** After a shooting near Hebron in which two Israeli soldiers were killed, Israeli forces killed two Palestinian men. Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu ordered preparations for a full lockdown of the West Bank and complete closure of Hebron, and Israeli forces demolished buildings near Hebron and detained dozens in raids. The moves come days before Israeli elections on Oct 27. Expect further restrictions on movement and a risk of escalation through the vote. Source: https://www.timesofisrael.com/liveblog-october-23-2026/

**US threatens new strikes on Iran as oil surges:** President Trump has threatened fresh strikes on Iran, and US Ambassador to Turkey Tom Barrack said a strike "absolutely" could come as early as this weekend. UN Ambassador Mike Waltz told the Financial Times the US war on Iran could last another year. Brent crude futures settled at $116.72 a barrel on Oct 23, up about 3%. A new round of strikes would likely push energy prices and inflation pressure higher worldwide. Source: https://www.democracynow.org/2026/10/23/headlines

**Russia pounds Kyiv; US announces local pause in Oleshky:** Russia fired roughly 210 drones and ballistic missiles at Kyiv overnight; Ukraine downed 181 drones but intercepted none of the ballistic missiles, and officials reported deaths and dozens of injuries in the capital. Separately, the US announced a localized arrangement to suspend fighting in the Russian-held town of Oleshky in Kherson region to allow aid deliveries and civilian evacuation. It is not a wider ceasefire, and its durability will be an early test of US-brokered de-escalation. Source: https://www.fdd.org/overnight-brief/october-232026/
