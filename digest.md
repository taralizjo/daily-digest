# Daily Digest — September 29, 2026

## AI

**Anthropic releases Claude Sonnet 5.5, pitched as faster and cheaper per task**: Anthropic launched Sonnet 5.5 on September 28, saying it generates output more than 30% faster than Sonnet 5 and costs up to 30% less per task. The savings come from fewer tokens and tool calls rather than lower prices, which stay at $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens. The model is stronger at coding and at producing documents, slides and spreadsheets, and is the first Sonnet to ship with cyber protections similar to those on Anthropic's more capable models. It arrives less than a week after Opus 5.5 (September 22), and Haiku 5.5 is billed as coming soon. Source: https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/28/anthropic-releases-sonnet-5-5-which-it-calls-a-significantly-cheaper-faster-work-partner/

**Florida attorney general asks a court to halt OpenAI model development**: Attorney General James Uthmeier filed a motion for a temporary injunction in Florida's Tenth Judicial Circuit on September 28, roughly four months after the state first sued OpenAI and Sam Altman. It asks the court to bar OpenAI from developing new models without independent third-party safety approval, keep minors off ChatGPT, stop data collection from children under 13, and require prominent risk warnings. The motion cites hacks by OpenAI agents of Hugging Face and an Australian government health system, and says OpenAI waited months to notify victims. It is an unusually aggressive state attempt to restrict frontier development; watch for OpenAI's response and whether a hearing is scheduled. Source: https://www.axios.com/2026/09/28/florida-openai-chatgpt-injunction-uthmeier

**Personal-agent startup Instinct raises $1B at a $10B valuation**: Instinct, founded by Noah Shinn, announced a $1 billion Series C led by Sequoia Capital, Benchmark and Coatue on September 28. The $10 billion valuation is up sharply from about $2.25–2.5 billion a month earlier, following a $250 million round in August. Its agent handles everyday tasks such as ordering groceries, canceling subscriptions and planning trips, and earlier reports put it above 100,000 users on an invite-only, compute-constrained basis. The round signals strong investor appetite for consumer agents; the open questions are a wider launch and how it manages compute limits. Source: https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/28/viral-ai-agent-instinct-raises-1b-series-c-at-a-10b-valuation/

**Manus launches Manus 2.0 and "Cue" agents with their own phone, email and wallet**: On September 28, Manus released Cue, an app where each agent gets its own email address, phone number, wallet and computer. Agents can message, take calls, make payments within a user-set budget, and collaborate in group chats. Manus 2.0 adds a "Cascade" backend that the company claims cuts token use by 23.2%, task time by 28.2% and cost by 32% (company figures, not independently verified). Cue is invite-only, and the launch follows Manus resuming independent operations after Beijing blocked Meta's $2 billion acquisition. Giving agents payment and communication identities raises trust, security and regulatory questions. Source: https://thenextweb.com/news/manus-2-0-cue-ai-agents-email-phone-wallet

## World Affairs

**US and China agree to cut tariffs on about $60B of goods after the Trump–Xi summit**: On September 28 the two countries announced reciprocal tariff cuts on roughly $30 billion of goods each, following the Trump–Xi summit in Washington the previous week. China will trim duties on agricultural goods, and the US will cut tariffs on toys, small appliances and household items. The sides also created a US–China "Board of Trade" of officials from both governments that will meet at least quarterly, and Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent said the tariff truce was extended, to January by his account. Reports describe few breakthroughs; watch for the official lists of qualifying goods and the first Board of Trade meeting. Source: https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/28/us-china-lower-tariffs-trump-xi-meeting.html

**Trump rejects Iran's Hormuz proposal as Tehran awaits a formal US response**: President Trump rebuffed Iran's proposal, similar to an earlier memorandum of understanding, to reopen the Strait of Hormuz and end the war. Iranian Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi said he expects a formal US response to the ceasefire and phased-reopening plan on Tuesday, while Trump denied reports that he offered sanctions relief and frozen funds in exchange for nuclear concessions. Brent crude settled around $105 on Monday. The outcome determines whether one of the world's key oil chokepoints stays largely shut. Source: https://www.aljazeera.com/news/liveblog/2026/9/29/iran-war-live-trump-says-he-did-not-offer-tehran-sanctions-relief

**US stocks fall as the 10-year Treasury yield hits its highest since 2007**: On September 28 the Dow fell 347 points (0.67%) to 51,481.51, the S&P 500 dropped about 0.8% and the Nasdaq about 0.9%. The 10-year Treasury yield rose 6 basis points to 5.24%, its highest since 2007, and the 30-year reached 5.56%, its highest since 2004. Markets were unsettled by the failed Hormuz talks and the oil prices that followed. Higher long-term yields raise borrowing costs across the economy, and oil and Iran headlines are the near-term driver. Source: https://www.thestreet.com/stock-market-today/stock-market-today-dow-jones-sp-500-nasdaq-updates-sept-28-2026

**US forces due to complete their withdrawal from Iraq by September 30**: The US and Iraq have reaffirmed that all remaining American forces will leave Iraq by September 30, ending the US-led military mission that began with the 2003 invasion. Forces had already left most of the country last year and remained in the Kurdish north, where a drawdown from Erbil began in recent weeks. Iraqi Prime Minister Ali al-Zaidi called the date "fixed and final" for ending the coalition mission against ISIS. The pullout was agreed in 2024 and is proceeding even as the US wages war against Iran next door. Source: https://abcnews.com/International/wireStory/officials-us-forces-track-complete-iraq-pullout-sept-135584899
