# Daily Digest — October 7, 2026

## AI

**AI labs testify under oath before the NYC Council:** On October 5, all 51 members of the New York City Council held a Committee of the Whole hearing on AI agent safety, with sworn testimony from Anthropic's Logan Graham, OpenAI's Morgan Dwyer, Google's Alice Friend and Meta's Shane Cahill. The executives said that eliminating risk or promising perfection isn't possible, and Speaker Julie Menin voiced frustration at non-answers on liability. Menin's proposed bill would bar selling or deploying an AI model in the city unless an outside validator has reviewed it and a human can shut it down. It matters because a city, not Washington, is the first US legislative body to put these labs under oath, and the validator and kill-switch bill is the one to watch. Source: https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/05/anthropic-openai-google-meta-execs-testify-nyc-council-ai-hearing.html

**Trump taps DNI Jay Clayton as AI czar:** Axios reported on October 2 that President Trump would name Director of National Intelligence Jay Clayton to lead federal AI policy while keeping his intelligence post, and Nextgov has since covered expert reaction to the move. Separately, on October 6 Senators Elizabeth Warren and Richard Blumenthal pressed the White House to release its still-unpublished AI regulatory framework (Semafor). The appointment sets who will shape federal AI rules, and the fight over the unreleased framework is the next flashpoint. Source: https://axios.com/2026/10/02/trump-new-ai-czar-jay-clayton-dni

**Pentagon reportedly stops using Anthropic products:** Multiple outlets report that a Defense official said on October 5 that the Pentagon has ceased using Anthropic products; I could not open the original BBC report, so treat the attribution as secondhand. Defense Secretary Hegseth designated Anthropic a supply-chain risk on March 3 with a six-month phase-out, after a roughly $200M contract collapsed over Anthropic's refusal to allow mass surveillance or autonomous-weapons uses. A district judge ruled the designation unlawful in August, but an appeals court later upheld it 2-1, and Anthropic says it is considering all options. The phase-out window is now ending, and further review of the designation is the likely next step. Source: https://thenextweb.com/news/anthropic-pentagon-supply-chain-risk-appeals-court-ruling

## World Affairs

**Israel marks three years since October 7 as the anniversary becomes an election issue:** Israelis held ceremonies, memorials and protests at more than 400 locations on the anniversary of the 2023 Hamas attack. The date falls three weeks before the October 27 election, the first since the attack, and Prime Minister Netanyahu and his opponents are both making it central to their campaigns. Reports that the UAE's Mohammed bin Zayed and Egypt's intelligence chief Abbas Kamel warned Netanyahu beforehand have driven the accountability debate; Netanyahu denies receiving any warning and has sued over the claims. Source: https://www.cnn.com/2026/10/06/world/october-seven-attack-anniversary-israel-intl

**G7 oil and diesel stock release works through markets:** G7 leaders agreed on October 2 to release up to 100 million barrels of emergency oil and diesel through the International Energy Agency over four months, with most diesel volumes front-loaded into the first 20 days. The deal followed US pressure, including a threat of a US diesel export ban, as US diesel hit a record $6.52 a gallon on September 22 amid the Iran war. Members also pledged to avoid export restrictions, and diesel futures fell more than 4% on the news. Whether the release holds fuel prices down will depend on the Strait of Hormuz and the state of US-Iran talks. Source: https://www.euronews.com/2026/10/02/g7-agrees-100-million-barrel-emergency-oil-release-amid-us-pressure-over-diesel

**S&P 500 closes at a record as oil and yields settle:** The S&P 500 closed at 7,818.93 on October 6, up 0.58%, with the Dow up 0.49% and the Nasdaq also higher. Oil prices and Treasury yields steadied after weeks of pressure from the Iran conflict, and Constellation Energy jumped more than 8% after signing a 3,590-megawatt power deal with Google. The record shows markets pricing in easing energy-supply stress, though it leaves them exposed to any renewed Gulf disruption. Source: https://thestreet.com/stock-market-today/stock-market-today-dow-jones-sp-500-nasdaq-updates-oct-06-2026
