# Daily Digest — September 23, 2026

## AI

**Anthropic launches Claude Opus 5.5, its cheapest frontier model yet, ahead of expected IPO**: Anthropic released Claude Opus 5.5 on September 22, pricing it at $4/M input and $20/M output tokens — about 40% cheaper than Opus 5 and roughly 30% faster on output, while performing comparably to Anthropic's higher-end Fable 5.1 on most tasks. It's the first of a new "5.5" family (Sonnet 5.5 and Haiku 5.5 are expected to follow) and is aimed at agentic coding, computer use, and knowledge work, shipping with "preserved thinking," an anti-distillation safeguard meant to stop competitors from extracting Claude's internal reasoning traces. The release comes as Anthropic reportedly prepares for an IPO and follows CEO Dario Amodei's public call just ten days earlier for the industry to slow down, drawing criticism for the apparent contradiction.
Source: https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-22/anthropic-unveils-more-cost-efficient-opus-5-5-model-before-ipo

**OpenAI launches GPT-6 Sol and GPT-6 Luna, cutting API prices in half**: On September 22, OpenAI released two new models — GPT-6 Sol (mid-tier reasoning) and GPT-6 Luna (fast, high-volume, cheapest tier) — extending the GPT-6 Astra family ahead of DevDay on September 29. Pricing dropped roughly 50% versus GPT-5.6 equivalents: Sol is $2/$10 per million input/output tokens (down from $4/$20) and Luna is $0.10/$0.50 (down from $0.20/$1.20), with OpenAI saying Sol makes "about half as many mistakes" as its predecessor. The models are rolling out across ChatGPT tiers, Codex and the API, signaling an intensifying price war with Anthropic's same-day Opus 5.5 cut.
Source: https://openai.com/index/introducing-gpt-6-sol-and-luna/

**OpenAI opens model training and evaluation to outside safety assessors**: OpenAI announced on September 22 it will let independent third-party organizations conduct technical safety assessments during model training and evaluation, not just before launch as before. The company is in talks with groups including METR and Redwood Research, with engagements expected to run from a few weeks to several months and principles covering evaluator independence, scientific rigor and security practices. It follows Sam Altman's September 12 pledge to give independent evaluators office access, equipment and publication rights, amid broader industry pressure for more rigorous frontier-model oversight.
Source: https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-22/openai-to-let-outside-groups-evaluate-ai-models-at-earlier-phase

**UN Security Council holds first high-level briefing on AI and international security, with Altman and Amodei**: On September 23, the UN Security Council convened a special session on AI and international security, called by France and chaired by Foreign Minister Jean-Noël Barrot. Briefers included OpenAI CEO Sam Altman, Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei, Yoshua Bengio (co-chair of the UN's Independent International Scientific Panel on AI) and Hugging Face CEO Clément Delangue, with Altman urging adoption of international benchmarks for measuring AI capabilities and safeguards. It's one of the first times frontier AI lab CEOs have briefed the Security Council directly on systemic risk, marking AI's move onto the formal international security agenda.
Source: https://theprint.in/world/openais-sam-altman-anthropics-amodei-to-brief-unsc-as-france-flags-ai-threat-to-kids-global-security/3050738/

**Alphabet's Intrinsic open-sources its core industrial robotics platform**: At ROSCon 2026 in Toronto on September 22, Alphabet-owned robotics company Intrinsic released Intrinsic Core, a ROS-compatible robotics software stack, under the permissive Apache 2.0 license. It bundles hardware-agnostic real-time motion control, pose estimation built on Nvidia's FoundationPose, collision-free motion planning and adaptive grasp planning — components previously kept proprietary — alongside a reference design for CNC machine-tending. Coverage described the move as an attempt to become "the Android of robotics," lowering the barrier for developers to build physical-AI applications without building control infrastructure from scratch.
Source: https://siliconangle.com/2026/09/22/googles-robotics-unit-intrinsic-open-sources-its-foundational-infrastructure-for-intelligent-robots/

## World Affairs

**Iran–US: Trump threatens to "annihilate" Iran at UN, but sides hold "productive" sidelines talks**: At the UN General Assembly's opening session on September 22, President Trump told world leaders he faces a "big decision" — a deal with Iran or to "annihilate the Islamic Republic." Hours later, Iranian Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi met US special envoy Steve Witkoff for roughly three hours on the sidelines; Iran set conditions for reopening the Strait of Hormuz (lifting the US naval blockade, releasing frozen assets, ending the war on all fronts), while Witkoff called the talks "promising, constructive." On September 23, Iranian President Masoud Pezeshkian responded defiantly, saying Iran will "never bow our head," prompting a US delegate to walk out. This is the most significant geopolitical flashpoint of the day given the active US blockade of the Strait of Hormuz, roughly 20% of global oil and LNG transit.
Source: https://www.cnn.com/2026/09/23/world/live-news/united-nations-general-assembly-iran-trump-ukraine

**Trump and Zelensky hold 40-minute UN meeting on Patriot missile licensing amid deadly Russian barrage**: On September 22 in New York, Trump and Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky discussed a possible energy ceasefire, US licensing to let Ukraine produce Patriot interceptor missiles domestically, continued Ukrainian strikes on Russian oil refineries, and the state of negotiations with Moscow. The talks came as Russia bombarded at least four Ukrainian regions overnight with missiles and drones, killing at least five people and hitting Dnipro, Kryvyi Rih, Pavlohrad, Kramatorsk and Zaporizhzhia; Ukraine struck another Russian refinery in retaliation. This signals a potential shift in US policy toward enabling indigenous Ukrainian air-defense production even as front-line casualties continue.
Source: https://meduza.io/en/news/2026/09/22/trump-says-talks-in-nyc-with-zelensky-will-cover-russia-ukraine-negotiations-and-patriot-interceptor-production-in-ukraine

**UN General Assembly's 81st session opens with ~130 heads of state amid overlapping wars**: High-Level Week (September 22–29) began Tuesday under the theme "Restoring trust, managing transformation," with outgoing Secretary-General António Guterres opening his final General Assembly amid faltering diplomacy on both the US-Iran war and Russia's invasion of Ukraine. Nearly 130 heads of state and government are speaking, including Trump, Zelensky, Macron and European Commission President Ursula von der Leyen, with the debate running through September 26. British PM Andy Burnham used his inaugural UN address to pledge UK leadership on global AI standards — making this the year's largest convening of world leaders and the venue driving nearly all of the day's major geopolitical news.
Source: https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/23/unga-trump-iran-china-greenland-ukraine-russia-.html

**DoorDash pays record $131.5 million settlement to New York City over underpaid delivery workers**: On September 22, DoorDash reached a $131.5 million settlement with New York City's Department of Consumer and Worker Protection following an investigation into systematic underpayment. The deal includes over $115 million in direct payments plus about $16.7 million in civil penalties, with $83 million resolving how pay was calculated for workers logged into the app but not actively delivering. More than 260,000 NYC delivery workers will receive compensation (median payout about $48); city officials, including Mayor Mamdani's office, called it the largest worker settlement in New York City history and a major precedent for gig-economy labor enforcement nationally.
Source: https://www.nbcnews.com/business/business-news/doordash-settles-nyc-driver-pay-rcna599186

**Nigeria's central bank cuts key interest rate to 23% amid easing inflation**: The Central Bank of Nigeria's Monetary Policy Committee, meeting September 21–22 in Abuja under Governor Olayemi Cardoso, cut the benchmark Monetary Policy Rate to 23%, reversing two prior holds at 26.5% in May and July and following an earlier cut from 27% in February. The move signals the central bank judges inflation pressures easing enough to begin loosening policy, a significant shift for Africa's largest economy and a bellwether for other emerging-market central banks navigating post-pandemic disinflation.
Source: https://www.premiumtimesng.com/business/business-news/911533-just-in-cbn-cuts-interest-rate-to-23.html
