## AI

**Sweden's Government Offices block ChatGPT and Claude on staff computers**: Sweden's Government Offices (Regeringskansliet), which support the prime minister and cabinet, began blocking ChatGPT and Claude on staff work computers on Monday, Oct 26. Microsoft Copilot is the approved alternative and passed an internal security review only after its settings were tightened. Sveriges Radio's Ekot broke the story on Oct 23, quoting digitalisation director Mikael Wettercrantz's intranet note that using AI services without a formal contract could affect information security. A limited number of staff can still reach both models through an in-house AI platform. It shows governments tightening control over consumer AI tools and favouring vendors under contract; reports differ on which other agencies are affected. Source: https://decrypt.co/381763/sweden-government-offices-block-chatgpt-claude ; https://cryptobriefing.com/sweden-government-blocks-chatgpt-claude-copilot/

## World Affairs

**Israel votes in its first national election since Oct 7**: Israelis went to the polls on Tuesday, Oct 27. Final pre-election polls had ex-army chief Gadi Eisenkot's Yashar party narrowly ahead of Netanyahu's Likud (Kan 11: 22 seats to 20), with the opposition bloc projected at 52–54 seats against about 50 for Netanyahu's coalition. Neither side looks sure of the 61 seats needed, and the opposition may need Arab parties' support, so coalition talks could run for weeks. No results or exit polls were confirmed at the time of writing.
