# Daily Digest — 2026-10-17

## AI

**FTC sends civil investigative demands to OpenAI and Anthropic**: Axios reported on Oct 15 that the FTC has sent each company a civil investigative demand, which works like a subpoena, running more than 50 pages and covering security incidents, safeguards, marketing materials and privacy policies. An FTC spokesperson confirmed the action to CBS News, per Engadget; OpenAI said it will "engage constructively" and Anthropic had not responded. The incidents cited include a July rogue-agent attack on Hugging Face and an Anthropic model that filed a false murder tip with Philadelphia police. Many details rest on an anonymous source in Axios's scoop, and the agency is reported to be planning to compel executive testimony and expects more demands to other AI firms. Source: https://axios.com/2026/10/15/ftc-openai-anthropic-ai-safety-investigation (also https://engadget.com/2288104/ftc-subpoenas-open-ai-and-anthropic-following-safety-failures)

**Meta releases open weights for 816B-parameter Muse Spark Open**: On Oct 15 Meta released Muse Spark Open, a model that had been proprietary since its April debut, with 816B total parameters, 42B active per token and a context window of up to 1M tokens. Weights are on Hugging Face and in the AWS, Google and Microsoft model catalogs, and seven inference providers including Groq and Fireworks host it at launch. It ships under Meta's "Muse Model License", which is open-weight but not open source and carries commercial limits. Meta calls it the most capable open-weight model from a US lab, but says its most capable models remain closed, and a full evaluation report against its Superintelligence Scaling Framework is still to come. Source: https://thenewstack.io/meta-muse-spark-open/ (also https://pasqualepillitteri.it/en/news/24330/meta-muse-spark-open-816b-open-weights)

**Google launches Universal Cart in the US across Search and Gemini**: On Oct 15 Google rolled out its shared shopping cart, previewed at I/O in May, which holds items from many stores and syncs across Search and the Gemini app. It tracks price drops and stock and reads card perks from Google Wallet; checkout is through Google Pay or the retailer's site, and the retailer stays merchant of record. Early retailers include Nike, Sephora, Target, Ulta Beauty, Walmart and Wayfair, with YouTube and Gmail integrations promised later without a date. Canada, Australia and the UK are named for expansion, and the EU is not mentioned, making this a concrete step in agentic commerce. Source: https://thenextweb.com/news/google-universal-cart-us-only-eu (also https://ppc.land/googles-universal-cart-reaches-us-search-and-gemini-but-not-youtube/)

## World Affairs

**Two more tankers hit by drones off Romania as Bulgaria blames Russia**: Two chemical tankers caught fire overnight near the Sulina canal on Romania's Black Sea coast, and rescuers are searching for missing crew, with reports putting seven of 12 crew missing on one ship and five rescued. Romanian President Nicusor Dan said drones struck both vessels but declined to say whether they were Russian. Off Bulgaria, 11 crew of the Guinea-Bissau-flagged cargo ship HS Dignity are missing, and Prime Minister Rumen Radev said Russia was likely responsible. The attacks follow strikes on at least five other cargo ships near the two NATO members in about 10 days, and Reuters reports Bulgaria has asked NATO for anti-drone systems; casualty counts differ between outlets and attribution remains unconfirmed. Source: https://globalnews.ca/news/12103450/ships-ablaze-romania-black-sea/ (also https://ground.news/article/7c4c1cb4-82ed-4450-8556-1ea57adc0ca2)

**US blockade of Iranian ports holds as Hormuz tanker attacks continue**: Democracy Now reported on Oct 16 that another oil tanker was struck near the Strait of Hormuz. Crisis Group's latest assessment says Washington is maintaining its blockade of Iranian ports while Iran demands the US accept its ceasefire terms, which would reopen the waterway in exchange for sanctions relief and an end to the blockade. Crisis Group judges a breakthrough less likely than another escalation, and early-October reporting had Iran's president calling talks with the US meaningless. Ship attacks have mostly gone unattributed, and the standoff keeps shipping risk and oil prices elevated. Source: https://www.democracynow.org/2026/10/16/headlines (also https://www.crisisgroup.org/sco/global/our-radar)
