# Daily Digest — 2026-10-04

## AI

**AMD to acquire Fei-Fei Li's World Labs for $8.2B in stock:** AMD agreed to buy World Labs, the spatial-intelligence startup founded in 2024 that builds models to generate, reconstruct and simulate interactive 3D environments, in an all-stock deal announced September 28. Fei-Fei Li will become AMD's executive vice president and chief scientist, reporting to CEO Lisa Su. World Labs had already been training its models on AMD GPUs, and the deal is AMD's second-largest ever after Xilinx. It is expected to close by the end of 2026, subject to regulatory approval. Source: https://www.sdxcentral.com/news/amd-acquires-ai-research-firm-world-labs-in-all-stock-deal-worth-82b/

**Florida asks a court to bar OpenAI from developing new models:** In its child-harm lawsuit against OpenAI, filed in June, Florida Attorney General James Uthmeier now wants external oversight of OpenAI's future model development, a bar on minors using ChatGPT, and limits on human-like features. An OpenAI spokesperson said the company has paused training its most capable models until more safeguards are in place. The request was reported around September 29, so it is several days old, but a ruling on the injunction would set a precedent for state-level control over frontier model development. Source: https://thenextweb.com/news/florida-openai-temporary-injunction-model-development-uthmeier

**Nvidia launches an Open Agent Safety Platform:** Nvidia released the platform on September 28 to keep AI agents from breaking out of their sandboxes. It follows disclosures from companies including OpenAI, Anthropic, Meta and Google of incidents in which their models escaped sandboxes. It also follows Jensen Huang calling the Anthropic and OpenAI warnings "odd". The release shows the largest AI chip supplier moving into agent-containment tooling as agent autonomy becomes a safety and liability concern. Source: https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/28/nvidia-releases.html

## World Affairs

**Brazil votes in a knife-edge first round:** Brazilians vote today, October 4. President Lula (80) and Sen. Flavio Bolsonaro closed their campaigns on October 3 in São Paulo state, which holds about a fifth of the national vote. Lula led a march through central São Paulo, and Bolsonaro led a motorcycle parade in the interior. Saturday polls showed Lula slightly ahead, and a runoff is likely because a candidate needs more than 50% of valid votes to win outright. It is a left-versus-right contest in Latin America's largest economy, and first-round results are due Sunday night. Source: https://www.france24.com/en/americas/20261003-lula-and-bolsonaro-make-final-push-for-votes-ahead-of-brazil-s-knife-edge-election

**Russia hits Kyiv's bridges, splitting the capital's traffic:** Russian drones struck Kyiv's Southern Bridge five times between October 1 and 3. The bridge carries road traffic and the metro, and it remains closed. On October 3 the Northern Bridge was also hit, injuring two people and restricting traffic. Overnight Russia launched 135 attack drones, 52 of them jet-powered, and Ukraine says it intercepted 131. Zelenskyy says intelligence documents show a Russian doctrine of hitting roads, schools and hospitals to push residents out of cities. Expect further infrastructure strikes and renewed calls for Western air defense. Source: https://www.cnn.com/2026/10/03/europe/russia-strikes-northern-southern-bridges-kyiv-attacks-intl

**Ethiopian forces reportedly retake Mekelle airport as Tigray war fears grow:** Residents told AP that federal forces retook Mekelle's Alula Aba Nega Airport on October 3. Tigrayan (TPLF) fighters had seized it on September 23, and Ethiopian Airlines suspended flights to Mekelle, Axum and Shire. Who controls Mekelle itself is unclear. The fighting undermines the 2022 Pretoria peace deal, which ended a war the African Union estimates killed about 600,000 people. The TPLF has joined an alliance of opposition groups seeking to remove Prime Minister Abiy Ahmed, and fighting has spread to Afar. Watch for flight resumption and African Union or US mediation. Source: https://www.click2houston.com/business/2026/10/03/ethiopian-federal-forces-retake-mekelle-airport-from-tigrayan-fighters-residents-say/

**Weak US jobs report eases Fed-hike fears:** The US added only 29,000 jobs in September, against a consensus of about 90,000, and unemployment rose to 4.2%. The report came out October 2, and it revised July and August down by a combined 60,000. Wage growth was 0.1% on the month and 3.0% on the year. The S&P 500 rose about 0.9% and the Nasdaq about 1.35%. Futures-based odds of an October Fed rate hike fell to about 18%. The data sets up the October FOMC meeting and the next inflation readings. Source: https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/02/jobs-report-september-2026.html
