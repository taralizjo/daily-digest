## AI

**Anthropic circulates a draft federal AI-safety bill**: Anthropic has privately circulated draft legislation that would limit when federal authorities can halt an advanced AI model. Action would require a foreseeable, imminent risk of more than $1 billion in damage or of killing or seriously injuring more than 50 people, tied to a biological weapon, a model evading its developer's controls, or a cyberattack. Commerce could not shut a model down itself; it would have to seek an order from a federal judge, and independent evaluators registered with Commerce would have to report imminent threats within 72 hours. It is only a draft with no announced congressional timetable, and critics are focused on the dollar threshold and the limits on federal enforcement. It overlaps with the Senate Democrats' AI Risk Management and Security Act of 2026. Exact date of the reporting could not be confirmed as within 24 hours. Source: https://interestingengineering.com/ai-robotics/anthropic-confidential-congress-draft-bill

**Altman says OpenAI will build a humanoid robot**: Sam Altman answered a question about a humanoid robot with a short "yes" on the Sources podcast and said OpenAI would also build other form factors. He told Fortune he expects a demo in 2027, with robots on city streets a few years after that. One outlet counts 27 robotics openings on OpenAI's careers page, including roles tied to custom actuators and manufacturing. It signals a move into hardware and embodied AI against Tesla, Figure and others; no partner, price or release date has been named. Outlets disagree on when the remarks were made, so the timing is uncertain. Source: https://aiweekly.co/alerts/altman-confirms-openai-will-build-humanoid-demo-in-2027

## World Affairs

**France imposes curfews after nationwide riots**: Protests on Saturday, October 17 over living costs and school conditions turned into rioting and looting. Prime Minister Sébastien Lecornu called "localized" curfews on October 19, including 11pm–6am around Place de la République and Les Halles in Paris and 9pm–6am in part of central Lyon, with others in Lille and a curfew for minors in Nice. Authorities say about 3,000 businesses were damaged or looted and at least 2,400 people were arrested; Interior Minister Laurent Nuñez offered to resign and Macron refused. It is France's most destructive unrest in years and puts pressure on Macron and Lecornu over cost of living and school budgets. Watch whether curfews are extended or lifted. Source: https://cp24.com/news/world/2026/10/19/frances-prime-minister-calls-for-localized-curfews-in-cities-to-curb-rioting-and-looting

**Houthi missile strike kills at least 53 in Yemen's Shabwa province**: On October 19 the Houthis fired three ballistic missiles at a camp of a new government "Rapid Intervention" force while recruits were lined up for training. Shabwa's provincial government reported at least 53 killed and about 200 wounded, calling the toll preliminary, and the Houthis acknowledged the attack. It is one of the deadliest strikes since fighting resumed in July, with more than 250,000 people reportedly displaced since then. Expect a Saudi-backed government response and a rising casualty count. Source: https://scmp.com/news/world/middle-east/article/3371416/houthi-attack-kills-53-yemen-government-training-camp

**US proposes new Russia–Ukraine talks and Patriot licensing**: Volodymyr Zelenskyy said the US has proposed new technical-level talks among Ukraine, Russia and the US, with the UAE as the venue, and that Kyiv is awaiting a date and expects the sides to report back in about 10 days. He also said Trump made a "final decision" to license Ukrainian production of Patriot missiles, which Ukraine has been short of since the Iran war began. It is the first concrete diplomatic track in some time; watch whether Russia agrees to attend. The date of the remarks was not clear, so this may be older than 24 hours. Source: https://weartv.com/news/nation-world/ukraine-president-zelenskyy-says-united-states-president-donald-trump-moves-to-restart-russia-ukraine-talks-backs-patriot-missiles-production-ending-war-negotiations-united-nations-putin-energy-infrastructure

**India's voter-roll purge sparks sustained protests**: A nationwide "Special Intensive Revision" of voter rolls, begun in Bihar in 2025, has struck more than 130 million names, and millions have appealed. Congress and the youth "Cockroach" movement have protested in New Delhi for over a week demanding the chief election commissioner resign, and Rahul Gandhi was briefly detained. The Election Commission says the revision removes duplicate and ineligible entries. It is a legitimacy dispute over the electoral process in the world's largest democracy; watch for court challenges.
