# Daily Digest — 2026-10-26

## AI

**Anthropic makes its S-1 public for a Nasdaq IPO**: Anthropic made its registration statement public on Oct 26, planning to list on the Nasdaq Global Select Market under the ticker "ANT", with Goldman Sachs and Morgan Stanley leading the underwriters. The filing reports about $16.3B of first-half 2026 revenue, up from roughly $1.16B a year earlier, and more than 1,900 customers paying over $1M a year. It also shows an $80.7B first-half net loss, mostly a non-cash charge tied to convertible notes. Price range and share count are still blank, and the WSJ (citing people familiar) reports a target of the week of Nov 16. This would be the first frontier-lab IPO, so its pricing will be a valuation test for the whole sector. Source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0002130357/000162828026067168/anthropicpbc-sx1.htm ; https://cnbc.com/2026/10/26/anthropic-ipo-live-updates-ai-s-1-filing.html

**Meta plans to raise up to $95B, mostly in stock, to fund AI buildout**: On Oct 26 Meta said it will raise up to $95B. About $35B comes first, made up of roughly $22.5B of Class A common stock and $12.5B of mandatory convertible preferred. A further $60B would follow through an at-the-market program starting in early 2027. Meta said Nvidia, AMD and Broadcom are interested in buying up to $5B of shares, and Semafor notes it is Meta's first new stock issuance since 2013. It shows AI capital spending is now being financed with equity, not just cash flow and debt. Source: https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/26/meta-stock-offering-ai.html ; https://www.irishtimes.com/business/2026/10/26/meta-plans-95bn-stock-offering-to-bankroll-ai-expansion/

**SpaceX secures a $40B Apollo-led commitment to finance AI chips**: Musk said on X on Oct 25 that SpaceX had closed $40B of investment-grade debt for GPUs, but a regulatory filing on Oct 26 describes only a binding commitment, with pricing and closing expected by year-end. Under the structure, an Apollo-managed entity would buy the chips and lease them to SpaceX on leases of a little over five years, with Goldman Sachs leading talks. Chips are expected to arrive in the first and second quarters of 2027. Morgan Stanley's Adam Jonas estimates up to another $230B of SpaceX debt in 2027–29, a sign of how much leveraged financing AI compute now needs. Source: https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/26/spacex-closes-40-billion-debt-deal-for-ai-chips-elon-musk-.html ; https://www.upi.com/Top_News/US/2026/10/26/SpaceX-AI-chips-finance-deal/6781793014680/

## World Affairs

**Lula wins a record fourth term in a surprise Brazil runoff**: Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva beat Senator Flávio Bolsonaro on Oct 25 with about 51.8% to 48.2% of valid votes on a near-complete count, a margin of roughly 4.4 million. Flávio had led the first round 47.0% to 45.2%, and most polls had him narrowly ahead in the runoff. Flávio says he cannot accept the result and will ask the courts to annul it, and Trump claimed without evidence that Brazil applied Biden's playbook. Lula's new term begins Jan 5, 2027, so a contested transition is the next risk. Source: https://www.npr.org/2026/10/25/nx-s1-6016299/brazil-lula-bolsonaro-election ; https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-10-25/lula-wins-a-record-fourth-term-in-brazil-in-a-surprise-comeback

**Brazilian markets plunge after Lula's win**: On Oct 26, B3 halted trading for 30 minutes after the Ibovespa fell more than 10% from Friday's close of 205,082.56. The commercial dollar jumped from R$4.993 to about R$5.60, a fall of roughly 12% in the real, and US-listed Brazilian shares also slumped. The selloff reflects investor surprise at the result and fear about fiscal policy. Watch for any central bank or government intervention and for contagion to other emerging markets. Exact percentages vary by outlet and time of day. Source: https://financefeeds.com/brazilian-real-falls-12-percent-to-r-5-60-ibovespa-circuit-breaker-after-lula-wins-runoff/ ; https://www.riotimesonline.com/brazil-stocks-halted-circuit-breaker-lula-win-2026/

**Serbia's Vučić concedes defeat to the student-led list**: In the Oct 25 snap parliamentary election, the Student List won about 51–52% in preliminary figures from Crta and Euronews, well ahead of Vučić's coalition. Vučić said, based on sample data, that the Student List would take about 135–140 of 250 seats and his bloc around 105. The list grew out of nationwide anti-corruption protests, and its win ends the dominance of Vučić's SNS, which has governed since 2012. Next come certified results and the formation of a government. Source: https://www.euronews.com/2026/10/25/vucic-concedes-defeat-in-pivotal-serbian-elections-says-student-movement-has-won ; https://www.cnn.com/2026/10/25/europe/serbian-strongman-vucic-concedes-defeat-to-student-led-movement-intl-latam

**Trump keeps renewed strikes on Iran on the table as Hormuz tensions persist**: Over the weekend of Oct 24–25, Trump said a renewed attack on Iran was possible "maybe tomorrow" while also saying Tehran wants a deal to reopen the Strait of Hormuz; on Sunday he said there would be no strikes that day. He claims oil flow through the strait now exceeds pre-war levels under the US naval blockade, and CENTCOM says more than 3,000 commercial vessels have transited with US support, which Iran disputes. A third US carrier group has been deployed to the Gulf. A decision to strike or to deal would move global oil prices. Source: https://www.thenationalnews.com/news/us/2026/10/25/trump-iran-strike/
